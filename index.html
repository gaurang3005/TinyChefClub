Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our workshops.

Fireless cooking involves preparing delicious dishes without using any fire, flame, or heat source. It includes activities like making sandwiches, fruit art, salads, no-bake energy balls, and other creative recipes that are completely safe for children.
Our workshops are designed for children of various age groups. We tailor our activities and recipes to be age-appropriate, ensuring that every child can participate, learn, and enjoy the experience fully.
Absolutely! We regularly conduct workshops at schools. Our team brings all necessary materials and equipment to the venue. We set up a clean, organized cooking space in classrooms or activity halls, making it convenient for schools to host our workshops.
Yes! Our cooking workshops make for a unique and memorable birthday party activity. Children love the hands-on experience, and they get to take home the treats they create. It's a fun, creative alternative to traditional party entertainment.
Our recipes range from sandwiches, fruit art, and salads to no-bake desserts, energy bites, and festival-themed treats. All recipes are fireless, easy to follow, and use fresh, child-safe ingredients. We regularly introduce new and exciting recipes to keep the experience fresh.
Safety is our top priority. All activities are completely fireless. We use sanitized utensils and equipment, ensure proper hand hygiene, work with fresh and quality ingredients, and maintain a clean cooking environment throughout every workshop. Our facilitators are trained to supervise children at all times.
